# DC provenance — every figure, and whether anyone has checked it

Generated by `dc_provenance.py` from the ledgers. Do not hand-edit: re-run it.

## Read this before the demo

**No District figure in this project is signed.** In this project *signed* means a named person opened the source and agreed with the number, and the register that records it (`signatures.py`) has no DC entry at all. Every figure below is therefore UNVERIFIED, including the ones that are measured, correct and uncontroversial. Measured says where a number came from. Verified says somebody looked. The second one has not happened for the District.

So the stipulation for the demo is not a hedge, it is the literal state of the data: **the DC figures are not proofread and not final.** The FY2030 panel is additionally a DRAFT — three of its seven HR-1 lanes carry no District figure at all and are drawn as absence.

What the status words mean here:

| word | meaning |
|---|---|
| MEASURED | read off a published source. Not checked by a person. |
| DERIVED | computed in code from other figures. No stronger than its parents. |
| MODELLED | **we chose it.** An assumption, declared. |
| ABSENT | there is no figure. Drawn as absence, never as zero. |

## What would change this

A figure becomes verified when someone opens its source, agrees with the number, and records it in `signatures.py` with the value they agreed to. Editing a signed figure makes the signature lapse and fails the build, which is the point of recording the value rather than just the name.

Until then the honest line for any audience is the one above: the District figures are sourced but unproofed, the assumptions are listed, and the absences are drawn rather than filled.
