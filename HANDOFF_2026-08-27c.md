## Structural change to the second ledger

D-47 supersedes D-40's structure. The second ledger is not a companion exhibit; it is
a property of one cell — government column, state row. The state general fund is an
**unsized container** with Medicaid's general-fund share as the one band inside it
carrying a number. Everything upstream and downstream stays Medicaid-only.

JW's rationale: a governor reading their own state edition wants their whole picture,
and Medicaid is one claimant among several — but only in that cell.

Leaving it unsized dropped the NASBO dependency, the state-fiscal-year conflict with
D-07, and the with-or-without-federal-funds trap, none of which were buying anything.
It also removes the headline risk, since an unsized container cannot generate
"Medicaid is X% of state budgets."
