person opened the source and agreed with the number, and `signatures.py`
records it as `verified = "YYYY-MM-DD initials"`. **It carries no District
entry at all.** Nothing below is signed, including the figures that are
measured, correct and uncontroversial.

`DC_QUESTIONS_FOR_JW.md`. Bracketed numbers key to the source register in
`DC_RESEARCH_2026-11-03.md`.

## Two defects in the repo that need a decision, not a signature

**D1.** The comment in `ledger_dc_2024.py` at the ASSUMED SERVICE MIX block
says the District's FFS dollar is "55 percent long-term care against about 20
percent nationally". On the repo's own constants the national FFS long-term
care share is **48.64%**; 20.48% is long-term care as a share of the national
hundred. The sentence compares a share of fee-for-service to a share of total
spending. Nothing drawn is wrong — it is a comment — but it is a sentence a
reader could repeat. The two correct forms are in `DC_RESEARCH_2026-11-03.md`
§9.1.

**D2.** `tobe2030_dc.DC_NAMED_PROVISIONS` says the legal-immigrant provision
is under a preliminary injunction. See 3.5 and Q11.
