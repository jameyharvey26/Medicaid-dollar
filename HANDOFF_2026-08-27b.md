## 2. The chain of reasoning, so it is not re-derived

1. CBO scores federal dollars; the $100 ledger is federal plus state, so D-25 grosses
   up by 1/FMAP.
2. That works for coverage-loss lanes. It fails for provider taxes, because the
   "state share" of a provider-tax-financed payment was the providers' own money
   recycled. Grossing up invents a state contribution that never existed.
3. Providers' real loss is the federal match. The rest is tax they stop paying, which
   has no destination among D-03's five nodes.
4. But provider tax revenue does two jobs. Where it financed payments back to the
   taxed class it recycles (80%). Where it substituted for state general fund it is a
   real state revenue loss (20%).
5. That 20% has nowhere to go on the first ledger, because it is not a Medicaid
   dollar stopping. It is a state fiscal consequence. Hence D-40.
6. Directed payments have the same structure with a third case: general-fund-financed
   directed payment cuts produce state **relief**, not pressure. Hence D-41 and D-42.

## 4. Two corrections that were carried into the repo

**D-26 sits on the outlay basis; `ramp.py` sits on the deficit basis.** Both of
D-26's illustrative figures reverse-engineer to $325.6B, CBO's federal outlay
reduction for §71119. `ramp.py` uses $317.0B, the deficit effect. An $8.6B wedge on
one section. This is evidence that the outlay-to-deficit gap is **attributable at
section level**, which is exactly what open task 2 needs to establish. Testable
hypothesis for the next session: the wedge concentrates in sections with revenue
interactions and is zero elsewhere. Several secondary summaries report §71101 at
$66.008B and §71102 at $53.6B under outlay-labelled columns, the same figures the
allocation treats as deficit effects, which would be consistent with that.

**The `ramp.py` provider tax weight of 0.40 blends two mechanisms.** Expansion states
face a scheduled phase-down with nursing facility and ICF/IID taxes exempt.
Non-expansion states are frozen, not reduced, and lose only against a rising
baseline. Different time profiles, one weight. Flagged in the file, value unchanged.
