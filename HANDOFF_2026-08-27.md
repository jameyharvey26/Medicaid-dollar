## 1. FY2029 allocation across CBO-scored sections — DRAFT, needs approval

Closes CBO's FY2029 deficit figure of **-$90.146B** by construction.

**Basis discipline (D-36):** section totals are *deficit* effects, so this allocates the
FY2029 *deficit* line, not the outlay line (-$92.944B). Mixing the two is the error this
project has made before. Conversion to an outlay basis for the $100 ledger is still an open
task.

### Where the ramp weights come from

All from statutory schedules described in CBO's own narrative — no separate source needed.

- **§71101, §71102 — 1.00.** Blocked on enactment (July 2025); at full effect well before 2029.
- **§71107 — 1.00.** Six-month redeterminations begin January 2027; full effect by 2028.
- **§71119 — 0.75.** States may impose from 1 Jan 2027, *must* by 1 Jan 2029. 2029 is the
  first universal year but still absorbing rollout and partial-year effects.
- **§71115 — 0.40.** Hold-harmless threshold steps 6.0% → 5.5% (2028) → 5.0% (2029),
  reaching 3.5% in 2032. FY2029 is two of five steps: 1.0pp of an eventual 2.5pp reduction.
- **§71116 — 0.25.** State-directed payments above Medicare rates cut 10 percentage points
  annually from FY2028. FY2029 is year two.
- **Other — 0.60.** Mixed bucket, assumed mid-ramp. Close to arbitrary; see limits.

### The finding this produces

**A 2029 diagram is a substantially different picture from a ten-year one, and the paper
should say so rather than leave readers to infer it.**

Provider taxes and state-directed payments are 37% of the ten-year money but only 20% of
2029, because both are barely started. Work requirements run the other way — 36% of the
decade, 42% of 2029. The two moratoria, which almost no one writes about, are 21% of 2029
between them.

### Limits — must be flagged on the artifact

1. The weights are Claude's reading of the statutory schedules, **not CBO's allocation**.
   Modelled, per D-37. CBO publishes annual detail in aggregate only.
2. **The 0.75 on §71119 is the softest number here and drives the largest lane.** It needs a
   sensitivity range before publication. Suggest 0.60 / 0.75 / 0.90.
3. The 0.60 on the residual is close to arbitrary — a mixed bucket with no single schedule.
4. Allocation is on a deficit basis. The $100 ledger is outlays. Not yet reconciled.

Reproduce with `ramp.py` (in this bundle): weights in `RAMP`, totals in `TEN_YR`.
