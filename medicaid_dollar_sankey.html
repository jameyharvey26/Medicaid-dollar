$100 Medicaid Dollars

$100 is the combined federal + state dollar. It comes together at the state, runs through the agency, is disbursed across three payer lanes, funds claims that fan out to six providers — then read as the share of each service consumed by each eligibility group. The tracker along the bottom follows the running balance as losses peel off. FY2024.

Reading it. Federal ($64.70) enters from above and merges with state ($35.30) into the $100 Medicaid dollar at the state agency. Two losses peel there: administration ($5.07) and Medicare premiums for duals ($2.90), which curve back left to the federal government (Medicaid paying Medicare). The remaining $92.03 is disbursed across MCO, dual-MCO and fee-for-service lanes. In the payer phase the capitated lanes give up $5.61 to plan administration and earnings (plan administration $4.85 — $3.81 non-dual MCO + $1.04 dual-MCO — plus public-company earnings $0.76, a subset). What remains ($86.42) funds claims, which fan out to six provider bars (sized by spend, layered by payer source); documented fraud ($0.15) peels off the bottom of the fee-for-service lane, leaving $86.27 of health services delivered. Each provider bar ends in a pie, sized to the bar, showing who consumes that service.

Conservation: 100 → −5.07 admin = 94.93 → −2.90 Medicare = 92.03 (= MCO 40.06 + dual 10.89 + FFS 41.08) → −5.61 plan admin & earnings = 86.42 to providers (node sum 86.43) → −0.15 fraud = 86.27 delivered. Every cut balances.

Draft notes. (1) FQHCs are merged into "Physicians & clinics" — the FFS FQHC line is real but MCO FQHC dollars sit unlabelled inside the capitated "professional" bucket, so a standalone FQHC node would understate it. (2) Behavioral health (~$9.51, ~11% of service dollars; literature 9.3–13%) is broken out as its own node from every line it occupies. The cross-line split is an estimate; T-MSIS claims would refine it. (3) "Wrap around services" ($13.33), now net of behavioral health, = dental + the CMS-64 "other acute" catch-all (NEMT, DME, lab/imaging, therapies, EPSDT/preventive, hospice, etc.); not itemised in the source. (4) Provider order is true size. (5) Plan administration $4.85 is split by lane in proportion to each lane's share ($3.81 non-dual MCO + $1.04 dual-MCO); public-company earnings ($0.76) is a subset of margin, estimated pending 10-K allocation. (6) Beneficiary pies are IPF-balanced to each service's user mix and to group totals — a re-expression of provider dollars, not a measured flow. (7) Dual capitation (~$10.89) is the softest payer figure. (8) Vintages: CMS-64 FY2024, HMA mix CY2021, group shares FY2023, duals CY2022.
