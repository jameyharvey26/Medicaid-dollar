### D-70 · The $100 is struck at the state agency, not at appropriation

The normalization point moves. $100 is the money budgeted to Medicaid at the
state agency: $950,164M for FY2024. The federal column may therefore carry more
than $100, and does — $65.29 federal and $35.47 state enter, $100.76 in total.

JW's reasoning, 18 September: the $100 is not a real quantity, it is a standard
for comparing like with like, and the definition can be expanded so long as
exactly $100 arrives at the agency.

Consequences, all of them intended:

- The blended dollar at the state agency is 64.53 percent federal, against 64.80
  percent of the appropriation. Those two numbers being different is the finding.
- Every figure downstream rises about 0.76 percent. Claims paid $86.42 → $87.08.
  Health services delivered $86.27 → $86.93. Fee-for-service $41.08 → $41.39.
- All 33 signatures taken on 16 September lapse and must be re-agreed.
- The 50 states and DC now sum to $99.33 of the national hundred, the territories
  $0.57, federal oversight $0.10. DC is 46.01 cents.

Admission test for anything drawn left of the node: money appropriated under the
Medicaid statute that is never cost-allocated to a state Medicaid agency.
Vaccines for Children is the only item in Exhibit 16 that qualifies.

### D-71 · Vaccines for Children peels in the federal column, terminating at CDC

$0.76, one hundred percent federal, $0 non-federal. It cannot peel at the state
agency because every other peel there comes off blended money and this money has
no state share; peeling it there would spend about 27 cents of state money on
vaccines no state buys. It peels before the blend and terminates at CDC.

It does not cross the lanes and does not reach providers. OMB allocates the funds
through CMS to CDC, CDC buys the doses under contract and distributes them free
to enrolled providers. Providers cannot be paid for the vaccine product at all.
The only Medicaid payment in the transaction is the administration fee, which is
already inside the $100 in physicians and clinics. See EN-49.

### D-72 · Administration splits

The $5.07 becomes state program administration $4.25 and federal oversight $0.10.
Vaccines for Children leaves the peel entirely under D-71. The oversight peel is
one arrow, not two: Fraud Control Units and survey and certification are $0.05
each, about a twentieth of one percent of the column, and would render as
hairlines. Both are named in EN-50.

This closes S-099 and corrects it. The residual was $4.32 administration plus
$0.76 vaccines. The $4.32 already had the two oversight lines folded inside it.
True state program administration is $4.22 of the old hundred, $4.25 of the new.

### D-73 · The tracker starts at $100.76

The tracker measures the whole run, so it begins where the diagram begins, and
the vaccine purchase is its first decrement. The node still reads exactly $100.
See S-102.
